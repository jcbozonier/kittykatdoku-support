Your quilt stays with you.

This privacy notice covers the KittyKatdoku iPhone game, browser game, and support website operated by Justin Bozonier.

Playing KittyKatdoku

The game does not collect or transmit your personal information to the developer. It has no accounts, advertising, tracking, analytics, chat, or online sharing. No camera, microphone, contacts, or location access is requested.

Progress, puzzle marks, preferences, and custom levels are saved locally on your device. These records are used to resume your game and are not uploaded to us. The game does not provide cross-device synchronization. Operating-system backups, if enabled by you, are controlled by your device and Apple account settings.

Your local saves

Saves remain on your device until removed. Deleting the iPhone app removes its local app data; offloading an app may retain data. In the browser, clearing the game’s site data removes local saves. We cannot retrieve or delete these local records remotely.

Opening our website

The game includes a link that opens this policy in your browser. Visiting the website is optional and is separate from offline gameplay. This website has no account system, advertising, contact form, or analytics scripts. Its images and styles are served from the same website; it does not embed third-party media or fonts.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Contacting support

If you choose to email support, Justin receives your email address, message, and attachments through Gmail. This information is used to respond to your request and troubleshoot the issue. Please send only what is needed to explain your question. To request deletion of a support message, contact bojuapps@gmail.com.

Purchasing the app

Apple processes App Store purchases. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple’s handling of purchase, account, and optional device diagnostic information is governed by Apple’s privacy policy and your device settings.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from players, including children, and has no advertising or social features.

Changes and questions

If the game’s data practices change, this notice and the App Store disclosures will be updated. Questions or support-data requests can be sent to Justin Bozonier at bojuapps@gmail.com.
