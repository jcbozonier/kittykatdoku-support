The basics

Every cat has a place.

Find the hidden cats by following the colored regions and the clues already sewn into your quilt.

One per color.

Each colored region contains exactly one cat. A color’s indicator shows when you’ve marked its cat.

Give them a row.

Cats never share a row or column. On puzzles with fewer cats than the board size, some rows and columns stay empty.

A little space.

Cats cannot touch, even at a diagonal. Use X marks to keep track of squares you’ve ruled out.

In the current playtest, click or drag to mark X; double-click or double-tap to mark a cat. Open Menu → How to play for keyboard and controller controls. Clues supplied with a level stay fixed.

Common questions

Let’s untangle it.

These answers describe the current playable build. The iPhone edition is in development.

Where is my saved game?

Choose Continue on the title screen to resume an unfinished puzzle. Progress, preferences, and custom levels are saved locally. In the browser, use the same browser and device; clearing site data can remove your saves. Cross-device sync is not currently available.

What do the hints and red X marks mean?

Cat and X hints are fixed clues supplied with the level. A wrong cat guess becomes a red X. Those red X marks and fixed clues remain when you reset the puzzle.

Why won’t my custom level save?

Each region needs exactly one hidden cat. Cats must occupy different rows and columns and cannot touch diagonally. Cat hints must match hidden cats; X hints must be on empty squares. Use a new level ID if the ID belongs to a built-in level.

The editor shows the specific problem when you save. Your unfinished draft can be reopened for editing.

The game says it couldn’t save. What now?

Open Menu and choose Retry save. Check that your device has available storage and that your browser permits site storage. Avoid clearing site data while troubleshooting. If the warning remains, email the exact message and your device details.

Can I quiet the kitten or reduce movement?

Open Menu to adjust music and sound effects, turn kitten sounds off, or enable Reduced motion. Choose Resume when you’re ready to return to the puzzle.

Is the iPhone version available?

The iPhone edition of KittyKatdoku is in development. There is no App Store download link yet. You can try the current browser playtest, which uses the earlier Kittydoku name and a landscape layout.
